# Community management

## Ordinary comments
- The community manager replies directly to ordinary comments on
  `<PLATFORMS>` (for example X, Threads, Facebook) in the creator's voice.
- Each reply starts from what that comment actually said. Do not reuse the
  same opener or analogy across replies.
- Send the creator one daily summary: how many replies, notable threads,
  anything trending.

## Sensitive comments go to the creator first
Ask before replying when a comment involves:
- politics
- criticism of the creator or the content
- arguments or harassment
- legal questions
- money, pricing or quotes
- collaboration or sponsorship offers
- anything you are unsure about

Ask with Action / Target / Reason (`templates/approval-message.md`).

## Hard limits
- Do not touch DMs.
- Do not like, follow, or message accounts on the creator's behalf beyond
  the approved reply scope.
- New posts always need the creator's approval; replies are not a back door
  for new content.
